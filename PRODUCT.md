# Product direction

Chess Intelligent is an academy-first learning intelligence product. Coaches need to understand
what evidence exists, inspect its lineage, and assign existing verified training. Students need a
clear next practice action and a plain-language reason.

The distinctive mechanism is the chain from canonical game and exact engine state to immutable
concept evidence, Skill Graph, training measurement, Academy workflow, and finally a validated
optional explanation. The first viewport should show that mechanism working, not a generic
analytics dashboard.

Task 016 preserves current production security, ontology immutability, and evidence semantics.
Grounded AI is optional and explanatory. The private pilot—not feature breadth—determines the next
product milestone.
