## THESIS

The public entry is an evidence desk, not a module launcher: one large academy dossier shows how a played game becomes a coach-visible decision, learning priority, and assigned next step.

## OWN-WORLD

Warm paper, deep forest, and amber form a master-game folio. Ruled evidence rows, chess notation, and a real board position carry the visual identity; generic analytics cards do not.

## STORY

A new Academy Owner understands the evidence-to-action loop in the first viewport, creates an account, creates an Academy, and reaches a calm guided empty state without choosing a role.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

An asymmetric editorial split keeps a concise promise and one dominant Start an academy action on the left. A large illustrative product composition on the right traces Game to Evidence to Priority to Training and leaves a visible cue into the next section.

## FORM

Persuade. Grounded structure 5, Annotated Dossier Spread, from concept seed 805515a0. The signature interaction highlights the same evidence lineage across the board position, coach note, and training action. All values are explicitly illustrative.

## FINISH

The result should feel like a serious coach opened a beautifully made working notebook: chess-native, measured, evidence-first, and immediately usable.
