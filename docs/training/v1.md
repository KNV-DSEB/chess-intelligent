# Adaptive Training policy V1

This is the reproducible Task 010 policy specification. Canonical constants and hashes live in `packages/domain/src/adaptive-training.ts`.

## Pinned plan identity

A successful plan is idempotent over the exact Skill Graph run, candidate policy/version/hash, item source policy, item generator version/hash, reveal policy, cooldown policy, and `maxItems`. Player and ontology must match the immutable Skill Graph. Policy/config or graph changes create a new plan.

## Candidate eligibility

Remediation requires all of:

```text
status = ESTIMATED
evidenceConfidence ∈ {MODERATE, HIGH}
masteryBand ∈ {EMERGING, DEVELOPING}
exact focal-player NEGATIVE decision source exists
```

Diagnostic eligibility is:

```text
status ∈ {NO_EVIDENCE, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE}
OR status = ESTIMATED and evidenceConfidence = LOW
```

It materializes only when an eligible positive Task 008 source exists. One insufficient negative event never becomes remediation. `ESTABLISHED` and stronger states receive neither V1 candidate type.

## Support and dispositions

Materialization support is limited to:

```text
tactics.discovered_attack
tactics.fork
tactics.pin
tactics.skewer
```

Each requires an explicit `training.attempt` policy in the pinned ontology. Candidate decisions remain auditable as `ELIGIBLE`, `NO_ITEM_SOURCE`, `UNSUPPORTED_CONCEPT_V1`, `ONTOLOGY_POLICY_UNSUPPORTED`, `PREREQUISITE_OBSERVED_NOT_READY`, or `RECENTLY_ATTEMPTED`, plus a machine-readable reason code.

## Prerequisite readiness

- No prerequisite: `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- Every prerequisite is `ESTIMATED`, at least `MODERATE`, and `ESTABLISHED` or stronger: `READY`.
- Any prerequisite is `ESTIMATED`, at least `MODERATE`, and `EMERGING`/`DEVELOPING`: `OBSERVED_NOT_READY`; defer the dependent.
- Missing, insufficient, or low-confidence prerequisite state: `UNVERIFIED`; annotate without blocking solely from absence.

The selector evaluates the finite ontology DAG once and never recursively duplicates candidates.

## Deterministic ordering

Eligible decisions sort before unavailable decisions; remediation sorts before diagnostic. Remediation ties use higher confidence, lower posterior mean, newer negative source date, then stable concept ID. Diagnostic ties use source availability, prerequisite readiness, lower evidence confidence, then stable concept ID. Ranks are ordinal integers, not false-precision scores. `maxItems` takes the first eligible materializable decisions.

## Source policy and cooldown

No external or unrelated global Game is queried. Remediation source is an exact negative contribution in the pinned Skill Graph. Diagnostic source is positive decision evidence by either side in the pinned graph's selected Games. All sources require compatible persisted Task 004 and Task 008 semantics.

The same Player/source evidence is suppressed for 14 days after an attempt under `TRAINING_COOLDOWN_V1`. Cooldown affects new selection, not historical evidence validity. Reusing the same immutable graph returns its existing plan.

## Generator and reveal

`TACTICAL_TRAINING_ITEM_GENERATOR_V1` creates only `FIND_BEST_MOVE`. It reconstructs exact state and checks history hash, legal move, fact move, target motif, concept, analysis compatibility, and generator config. It never searches for alternatives; one strongly supported move is sufficient.

Remediation reveals its guided-practice concept before scoring. Diagnostic hides concept, source, and solution until scoring. Both hide accepted moves and source Game/ply before the first scored attempt.

## Attempts and evidence

One legal submission creates one immutable attempt. `CORRECT` creates positive and `INCORRECT` creates negative `training.attempt` evidence. The pinned ontology resolves and persists the role. Illegal moves create neither record. Duration is stored but not weighted.

All retries remain visible. `TRAINING_EVIDENCE_SELECTION_V1` selects only the earliest scored attempt by attempt number, submission time, and ID for one Player + TrainingItem.

## Skill Graph V2

Game evidence is unchanged from V1. Training evidence uses:

```text
effective training mass
= persisted ontology role weight
× source weight
× EVIDENCE_RECENCY_V1
```

Source weights are `0.5` for personal remediation replay and `1.0` for diagnostic assessment. The posterior remains Beta(2,2).

Confidence uses `canonicalGameCount + selectedTrainingItemCount` as the independent evidence-unit count while preserving both counts and all four source/polarity masses separately. Training is additive; V1 rows and historical runs are never recomputed.
