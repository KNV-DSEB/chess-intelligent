# Classifier rules V1

This catalog is the auditable rule surface for `CONCEPT_CLASSIFIER_BUNDLE_V1`. Thresholds and geometry constants are centralized in `CONCEPT_CLASSIFIER_V1_CONFIGURATION`; its canonical JSON is SHA-256 hashed into every classification run.

## Tactical motif classifier

Classifier: `TACTICAL_MOTIF_CLASSIFIER` version `V1`. Evidence: `position.tactical_motif`, `NEUTRAL`, decision-scoped.

| Concept stable ID           | Rule ID                         | V1 condition                                                                                                                                                                   | Explicit exclusion                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `tactics.fork`              | `TACTICAL_FORK_V1`              | The moved piece attacks at least two enemy targets worth at least a minor piece. Without a king target, combined target value must exceed moved-piece value by at least three. | Pawn-only low-value attacks and insufficient material surplus.                                                           |
| `tactics.pin`               | `TACTICAL_ABSOLUTE_PIN_V1`      | A moved bishop, rook, or queen sees an enemy non-king piece followed by that enemy king on the same unobstructed ray.                                                          | Relative pins and vague value-based constraints.                                                                         |
| `tactics.skewer`            | `TACTICAL_KING_SKEWER_V1`       | A moved slider sees the enemy king followed by a meaningful enemy piece on the same ray.                                                                                       | Non-king front targets and low-value rear pawns. This ordering prevents pin overlap.                                     |
| `tactics.discovered_attack` | `TACTICAL_DISCOVERED_ATTACK_V1` | Moving a piece uncovers a different friendly slider onto an enemy king or a target worth at least a minor piece.                                                               | Irrelevant pawn targets. A king target sets `discoveredCheck: true`; it is not duplicated under a nonexistent stable ID. |

Piece values are pawn 1, knight/bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9, and king 100. Target squares and pieces are sorted before persistence.

## Position structure classifier

Classifier: `POSITION_STRUCTURE_CLASSIFIER` version `V1`. Evidence: `position.structural_feature`, `NEUTRAL`, position-scoped with no player subject.

| Concept stable ID                    | Rule ID                            | V1 condition                                                                                | Evidence granularity                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `pawn_structure.isolated_queen_pawn` | `STRUCTURE_ISOLATED_QUEEN_PAWN_V1` | The side has at least one d-file pawn and no friendly c- or e-file pawn.                    | One concept/side/position fact with sorted d-pawn squares.                                                                   |
| `pawn_structure.doubled_pawns`       | `STRUCTURE_DOUBLED_PAWNS_V1`       | At least two friendly pawns occupy one file.                                                | One concept/side/position fact aggregating all doubled files and pawn squares.                                               |
| `pawn_structure.passed_pawn`         | `STRUCTURE_PASSED_PAWN_V1`         | No enemy pawn is ahead on the same or adjacent file, using color-aware promotion direction. | One concept/side/position fact aggregating passed squares; currently protected passed squares are included as subtype facts. |
| `pawn_structure.pawn_majority`       | `STRUCTURE_PAWN_MAJORITY_V1`       | The side has more pawns on queenside files a–c or kingside files f–h than the opponent.     | One concept/side/position fact with deterministic `wing:friendly-enemy` values.                                              |

Raw open/semi-open file detection is available in chess-core for future ontology versions. It does not emit V1 evidence because ontology 1.0.0 lacks the exact admissible concept/policy combinations.

## Tactical decision classifier

Classifier: `TACTICAL_DECISION_CLASSIFIER` version `V1`. Evidence: `decision.classification`, decision-scoped, exact compatible engine run required.

`TACTICAL_DECISION_SOUND_APPLICATION_V1` emits `POSITIVE` only when the played move independently creates motif M and either matches the engine top root move or has mover-relative loss at most 20 cp.

`MISSED_TACTICAL_MOTIF_V1` emits `NEGATIVE` only when the engine top move independently creates motif M, the played move does not create M, and the played decision loses at least 75 cp or has `MATE_MISSED`/`MATE_ALLOWED` consequence.

An engine loss with no detected motif emits no concept evidence. A motif without compatible engine evidence remains neutral. Mate consequence alone never becomes mating-net evidence.

## Versioning rule

Changing a threshold, target definition, geometry rule, stable-ID mapping, evidence type, or polarity behavior requires an explicit classifier/config/bundle version change. Historical runs and their evidence are not rewritten.
