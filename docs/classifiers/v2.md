# Classifier rules V2

`CONCEPT_CLASSIFIER_BUNDLE_V2` retains all V1 geometry and decision thresholds and adds five
conservative facts: overload, interference, removal of defender, back-rank mate, and hanging
pawns. Exact rules and adversarial fixtures live in `packages/chess-core/src/concept-facts.ts`.

The position and tactical motif classifiers emit V2 identities. The decision classifier emits
positive or negative evidence only when the independently detected V2 motif is paired with one
compatible exact-history engine run. Neutral facts remain non-mastery evidence.

Changing geometry, thresholds, selected concepts, evidence types, or polarity behavior requires a
new classifier/configuration/bundle version. V1 remains explicitly selectable.
