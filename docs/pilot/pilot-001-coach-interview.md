# Pilot 001 Coach interview

Conduct at the end of each Pilot week. Record role and Academy-scoped participant code only; do not
copy private chess notes into telemetry.

1. Which Student review did you complete, and what decision did it help you make?
2. Were the coverage states and denominators understandable before the priorities?
3. For which concepts did you Agree, feel Unsure, or Disagree? What exact evidence changed your view?
4. Could you trace a priority or AI claim to a useful game/training observation?
5. Was plan creation and assignment selection clear? Where did you hesitate?
6. Did progress comparison communicate compatible evidence without implying causality?
7. When AI was unavailable or unhelpful, could you finish the same workflow without it?
8. What consumed the most Coach time this week?
9. What should be fixed before adding any new capability?

Close with one most-important problem, its frequency, severity, and a concrete observed example.
