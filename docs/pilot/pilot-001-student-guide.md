# Pilot 001 Student guide

1. Sign in with your User account and open **My training**. Your Player record is chess data, not a
   login identity.
2. Read the readiness message. A missing graph or limited coverage means the system needs more
   verified information; it is not a negative judgment.
3. Open an assigned Practice or Assessment item. The accepted move remains private until a scored
   submission.
4. Submit your move. The first post-assignment attempt is the V1 measurement; retries remain visible
   but do not become independent mastery samples.
5. Review the evidence explanation after scoring. Training evidence is separate from game concept
   evidence.
6. A grounded AI brief is optional. Rate a claim if shown. AI failure never blocks assigned training.

Contact the Pilot support owner if you see another Student's information, cannot access your own
assignment, or encounter an incorrect identity/consent state. Do not share passwords or raw tokens.
